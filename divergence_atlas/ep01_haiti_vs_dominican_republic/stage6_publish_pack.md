## YouTube description

```
Same island. Same rain. In 1960, by one IMF estimate, Haitians and Dominicans earned about the same.
Today the Dominican Republic produces about five times as much per person. Why?

In the 1780s, Haiti was the most valuable colony on Earth, built on a plantation system that worked about half a million enslaved people to death. After the only successful slave revolt in history, France sent warships and a bill for 150 million francs, and Haiti paid for its own freedom for roughly seventy years. Next door, the Dominican side was neglected, never got that bill, and kept a working state through its own dictatorship. This video follows the rain, the sugar, the bill and the strongmen to explain how one island split into two very different fates.
```
