# Channel Style Guide: "The ENTIRE History" Empire Series

A blueprint for making videos in the same format as *Fortune of Nations Animated* ("The ENTIRE History of the American Empire in 16 Minutes"). It copies the format, not the content: every script and image here is original.

---

## 1. The format at a glance

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Length | 15–17 minutes (about 2,300–2,500 words of narration at ~150 wpm) |
| Subject | One empire or great power per video, from its origin to today |
| Structure | 10–12 named chapters, YouTube chapter markers every 1–2 minutes |
| Visual base | A dark, photoreal satellite map of the region; the empire's territory is animated as glowing overlays that grow and shrink over time |
| Cutaways | Cinematic historical scenes (ships, battles, cities, documents) between map sequences |
| Voice | One calm, confident narrator; talks *to* the viewer ("Think about that."); dry wit; no shouting |
| Angle | Every episode is built on a **paradox** about the empire (the US "almost never calls itself an empire"; Britain was "a small island that ran a quarter of the world") |

---

## 2. Title formula

**The ENTIRE History of the [Name] Empire in [N] Minutes**

- Always capitalise ENTIRE.
- N is the runtime rounded *up* (15:52 → 16 Minutes).
- Future titles: Ottoman, Mongol, Roman, Spanish, Russian, Mughal, Japanese, Persian (Achaemenid), Portuguese, Chinese (Qing), Soviet, Aztec, Mali.

## 3. Description formula

> This is the entire history of the [X] Empire, from **[humble starting point]** to **[peak, stated with one hard number]**. [One-line paradox.] [One-line origin irony.] So how did [question 1], [question 2], and [question 3]?
>
> Chapters:
> 0:00 [Chapter 1]
> …

## 4. Chapter rhythm (for a ~16 minute video)

| Slot | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | 0:00–1:00 | The peak and the paradox. End with "This is the entire history of…" |
| Origins | ~1:00–2:15 | Small, weak, ignored. Why nobody expected this |
| Rise ×3 | ~2:15–6:15 | Three chapters, each about **one mechanism** of expansion (trade, war, religion, a company, a technology) |
| Golden age | ~6:15–9:15 | The empire at full strength, plus the bill that someone else paid |
| Peak | ~9:15–12:00 | Maximum map. Say the size and population numbers out loud |
| Cracks / fall | ~12:00–14:50 | The war, debt or revolt that breaks it |
| What's left | last ~1:15 | The empire's traces today, the open question, and a closing line that echoes the cold open |

## 5. Narration rules

1. Short sentences. One idea per line, so each line can hold one image (one "beat").
2. Every chapter turns on a hard fact: a date, a number, or a named person.
3. Use "Think about that.", "Look closely…", "And the strangest part?" as pivots, at most once per chapter.
4. Give the colonised and conquered names, numbers and agency. Don't let the map hide the people.
5. End on a question the viewer can argue about in the comments.
6. Fact-check every number. If a figure is disputed, say "around", "at least" or "by some estimates".

---

## 6. Visual style

**Style tag for maps:** Cinematic 3D satellite-map animation, photoreal NASA-style terrain, desaturated dark land and near-black oceans, the empire's territory as a translucent glowing overlay with a soft bright edge, thin gold labels in a clean serif font, gentle atmospheric haze at the horizon, 16:9 widescreen, ultra-detailed, documentary mood.

**Style tag for scenes:** Photorealistic cinematic historical documentary still, period-accurate costume and architecture, natural motivated lighting, muted earthy palette with one warm accent colour, volumetric haze, shallow depth of field, 16:9 widescreen, subtle film grain, ultra-detailed, serious documentary mood.

**Empire colour code (keep it the same in every episode):**
| Empire | Overlay colour |
|---|---|
| British | Crimson red |
| American | Steel blue |
| French | Royal blue |
| Spanish | Gold-yellow |
| Portuguese | Emerald green |
| Dutch | Orange |
| Ottoman | Deep green |
| Russian / Soviet | Dark red-brown |
| Mughal | Saffron |
| Rivals / others | Neutral grey |

**Shot mix:** About 60% map shots, 40% scene cutaways. Open and close on the map.

**Never show:** gore, identifiable modern real people in invented situations, or real brand logos. Atrocities are shown through aftermath, objects, empty spaces and documents, never through graphic violence.

---

## 7. Prompt format (one entry per narration beat)

```
**#. "Narration line"**
**Prompt:** [style tag lead-in]: [subject, setting, period detail]. [lighting/colour]. 16:9 widescreen, ultra-detailed, [mood].
**Camera:** [move]
**Lighting:** [key light]
**Mood:** [two words]
**Action:** [what animates]
```

## 8. Thumbnail formula

A satellite globe, half in shadow, with the empire's territory glowing in its colour. A big three-to-five-word claim in bold white condensed type ("RULED 1/4 OF EARTH"). One small, sharply lit object for scale (a crown, a coin, a ship). No faces.

## 9. Production checklist

- [ ] Paradox and title chosen
- [ ] Script 2,300–2,500 words, chapters timed
- [ ] Every number checked against two sources
- [ ] One prompt per beat; maps in the empire's colour
- [ ] Chapter markers pasted into the description
- [ ] Thumbnail follows the formula
